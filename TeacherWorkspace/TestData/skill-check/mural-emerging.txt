The Mural on 14th Street

I saw the mural on 14th Street. It is big and it is on the side of a building. There are some people painted on it and they are holding things.

The colors are orange and red. I like the colors a lot. My friend likes it too and she said it was cool.

It was painted a long time ago, I think in the seventies. There are a lot of murals in our neighborhood. This one is the biggest one that I have seen so far.

I would give it a good rating. It is nice to look at when you walk past it on the way to the bus stop.
